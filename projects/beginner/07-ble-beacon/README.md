# 🟢 Project 07: BLE Bluetooth Low Energy Proximity Beacon

> Turn your ESP32 into a standalone Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) advertisement transmitter. Broadcasts custom manufacturer data, device local name, service UUIDs, and measured TX power for proximity tracking via smartphone apps (nRF Connect).

---

## 📋 Overview
- **Category**: Beginner (⭐ 1/5)
- **Supported Boards**: Classic ESP32, ESP32-S3, ESP32-C3, ESP32-C6, ESP32-H2
- **Features**:
  - Broadcasts standard BLE non-connectable undirected advertisements (`ADV_NONCONN_IND`).
  - Configurable advertisement interval (100ms to 1000ms) to conserve battery.
  - Zero external wiring needed.
